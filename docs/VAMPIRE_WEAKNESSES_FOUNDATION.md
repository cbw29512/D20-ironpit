Foundation for Vampire Weaknesses semantic family.

Source-verified from 2014 MM for Vampire and Vampire Spawn.
- Forbiddance: cannot enter residence without invitation.
- Harmed by running water: takes 20 acid damage if ends turn in running water.
- Stake to the heart: incapacitated if wooden piercing weapon is driven into heart while incapacitated in resting place.
- Sunlight hypersensitivity: disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks while in sunlight; takes 20 radiant damage if starts turn in sunlight.
