# Standard Pregen Gear

Iron Pit uses a deterministic **standard adventurer** gear profile for pregens. The goal is to represent a normal experienced character without random treasure rolls or highly optimized item combinations.

## 2024 higher-level baseline

The 2024 higher-level starting-character guidance is the rarity ceiling used by this profile:

| Level | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Very Rare |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2–4 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 5–10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 11–16 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 0 |
| 17–20 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 1 |

Source: D&D 2024 Basic Rules, Creating a Character: Starting Equipment at Higher Levels; Magic Items.

## Iron Pit martial default

The deterministic combat-facing subset is intentionally conservative:

| Level | Primary weapon | Magic armor | Magic shield | Simple utility items |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1–4 | mundane | mundane | mundane | 0 |
| 5–10 | +1 | mundane | mundane | 1 |
| 11–16 | +2 | mundane | +1 if the build uses a shield | 1 |
| 17–20 | +3 | +1 | +1 if the build uses a shield | 2 |

Mundane armor should also improve normally with wealth and level. For example, a heavy-armor Fighter should not remain in starter armor forever when a higher-level character could reasonably own better mundane armor.

Two-handed, dual-wield, ranged, and unarmored builds do not receive unusable shield or armor bonuses merely to fill a slot. Their remaining rarity budget can be filled by simple class-appropriate utility items once those items are certified.

## Certification rule

A pregen may be labeled **RAW READY** only when every combat-relevant magic item on the card is represented by the runtime. Listing a +1 weapon while resolving it as mundane, listing magic armor without applying its AC, or listing an activated item without implementing its action/resource rules blocks certification.

Non-combat flavor/utility items may be listed without runtime automation when they cannot change a standard Iron Pit fight.
