## 2014 magical visual disguise / Sea Hag Illusory Appearance — source contract

- Source text is combat-relevant **indirectly**: a visual illusion obscures the true form required by *Horrific Appearance*. It must not be silently declared arena-neutral or treated as Advantage, Invisibility, AC, or a global Blinded condition.
- Exact 2014 card facts: use Action to disguise, owner Bonus Action to end, ends on death, physical contact exposes inconsistencies, and another creature may spend its Action for source-derived DC 16 Intelligence (Investigation) to discern a disguise. - **Approved 2026-10-20 tactic:** enter with source-declared Illusory Appearance as the creature's **one** free precombat buff, then dismiss it on its own first initiative turn using the printed **Bonus Action** and continue its normal legal Action. A creature earlier in initiative cannot retroactively trigger a turn-start save: only later eligible targets see the revealed true form on their turns. Use the universal action economy (no free Bonus Action), ordinary RAW initiative totals, and the separately locked source-scoped gaze aversion. A candidate binding and Python/browser runtime are in PR #982; no source admission until Investigation/detection, death expiry and webpage battle verification are covered. Keep `source:extra-action` and **237/327 admitted, 90 blocked** meanwhile.
