- Spellcasting ability is Charisma and the source's save DC is 14. The
  complete six-spell list, innate frequency, and printed line fragments
  must match; altered source data fails closed.
- Barkskin does **not** grant its higher AC without being cast.
  The source armor class is 11, or 16 only while Barkskin is active.
  The 1/day resource, Concentration and timed expiration use the common engine.

  The enchantment is not permanent; its 1/day resource is independent of
  Barkskin's daily use.
- Tree Stride has no legal destination trees on the fixed Pit battlefield.
  Speak with Beasts and Plants is communication-only. Magic Resistance
  reuses the universal saving-throw Advantage grant.
