# Iron Pit Arena Policy

Iron Pit intentionally simplifies battlefield geometry while preserving the combat rules that decide the fight.

## Standard arena assumptions

- Every fight continues until one side is defeated. No morale, fleeing, surrender, retreat, or kiting AI.
- The standard arena is flat and unobstructed. Climb speeds, swimming, and other terrain-only movement modes do not matter unless a future arena explicitly enables that terrain.
- If a side has at least two active combatants, ally-within-5-feet requirements are treated as satisfied. Downed, unconscious, or dead allies do not count.
- Melee-primary combatants close toward melee and never retreat after engagement.
- A melee-primary combatant with a legal ranged attack may make one opening ranged attack, then uses normal movement to close.
- A melee-primary combatant without an available opening ranged attack takes the Dodge action while moving its normal Speed toward melee.
- Dodge imposes Disadvantage on attacks against that creature until the start of its next turn. Future saving-throw support must also add the Dodge Dexterity-save benefit before save-based combat is certified.
- Ranged-primary combatants remain at range while they can. They do not kite away from an approaching enemy.
- Once adjacent, a combatant uses a legal melee option if one exists. A truly ranged-only combatant may continue making ranged attacks in melee with the normal Disadvantage.
- Movement toward the enemy is allowed; movement away from the enemy is not part of the standard policy.

## Readiness rule

A stat-block feature does not block standard-arena readiness when the arena assumptions make that feature unable to affect the fight. A missing attack, damage rider, defense, save effect, condition, resource, reaction, spell, recharge feature, or other mechanic that can change combat outcome still blocks readiness.

These are explicit Iron Pit arena assumptions, not changes to a creature's printed statistics.
