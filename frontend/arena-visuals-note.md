# 2014 Beta Arena Visual Intent

This beta pass keeps combat cards fixed in their slots while making the Iron Pit feel like an arena.

- Registered original/licensed combatant art is preferred when available.
- Reviewed procedural SVG portraits remain the fallback so every certified combatant has a visual.
- Movement, melee lunges, ranged projectiles, casting pulses, hit recoil, prone poses, and defeat states animate inside the card rather than rearranging the battlefield layout.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables nonessential motion.
- Final level 6+ weapon-dependent pregens follow the canonical magical-offense baseline in `docs/CANONICAL_COMBAT_BUILD_POLICY.md`.
