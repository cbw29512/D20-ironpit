    A pocket dimension

    A pocket dimension where heroes and monsters are drawn in to battle for the enjoyment of an audience.

    You choose the matchup. Then you watch the fight. Every result is verified D&D 5e — 2014 and 2024 / SRD.

    The laws of the Pit

    The Iron Pit binds every combatant to the same arena law. Fights stay rules-accurate D&D 5e. Inside the Pit there is no teleport, no plane shift, no summoning, and no vertical flight. A natural 1 ends the turn.

    • No teleport
    • No plane shift
    • No summoning
    • No vertical flight
    • A natural 1 ends the turn
